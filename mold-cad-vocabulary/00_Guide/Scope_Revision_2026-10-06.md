# Scope revision — supervisor clarification (2026-10-06)

This note supersedes the earlier project-plan assumptions that the vocabulary should choose one mold process domain before drafting and that the target layer remained undecided.

## Confirmed target

- Describe shape features and shape properties **within one part** in mold CAD.
- The vocabulary is independent of any particular CAD software. Official software help can document the labels and operations used by a product, but those operations do not define the project vocabulary by themselves.
- Assembly trees and part-to-assembly membership are outside the main task.
- The intended scope is mold CAD generally. A first batch may use a practical family of examples, but that is a pilot choice, not a permanent restriction to one mold type.

## First useful examples

- Local features: hole, counterbore, countersink, pocket, slot, rib, boss, fillet, and chamfer.
- Shape properties: draft angle, undercut, and wall thickness.
- Internal geometry: cooling passages and enclosed internal voids that an exterior image may not reveal.
- Feature-instance relations and documented design purpose, such as a rib intended to reduce warpage.

## References identified by the supervisor

- Vocabulary construction and governance: ANSI/NISO Z39.19 and ISO 25964.
- Structure examples: MeSH and ICD-10.
- Technical terminology and feature context: STEP AP 224 and JIS drawing terminology.

The first research question is what each reference can contribute to a small, traceable feature vocabulary. None is to be copied as a complete taxonomy. See [[Vocabulary_Method_Comparison]] and [[Controlled_Vocabulary_Governance]].

The earlier v4 plan remains as project history; where it conflicts with this clarification, this revision and the working policy take precedence.
