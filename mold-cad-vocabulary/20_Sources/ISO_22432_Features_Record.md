## Collected scope

| Item | Official abstract says | Project use | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geometrical features | The standard defines general terms and types of geometrical features used for workpiece specification. | A possible reference when separating a feature's geometry from a specification or verification target. | Its scope is geometrical specification and verification, not a complete list of mold-CAD features. |
| Relationship map | ISO describes it as a roadmap for relationships among geometrical features and future consistent standards. | Check whether project categories confuse a shape, a measured feature, and a specification feature. | Do not claim that our relationships follow ISO 22432 without reading the relevant clauses. |

## Project decision

Use it as a category and boundary reference. Keep Hole, Rib, and similar project concepts as draft vocabulary concepts; do not replace them with standards terminology based on the abstract alone.
