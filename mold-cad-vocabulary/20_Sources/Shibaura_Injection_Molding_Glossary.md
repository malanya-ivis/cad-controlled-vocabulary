## Collected terminology

| Original label | Section / figure | Usage in context | Candidate concept | Open question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| キャビティ | キャビティ | Means the space between female and male mold portions where the product is formed; the same entry also says it may mean the female portion that forms that space. | [[Cavity_(injection_molding)]] | This is a documented ambiguity in one source. Decide whether the project needs two scoped senses. |
| コア | コア | A male mold part that forms the inside of the product, or a mold part used to form an undercut. | [[Core_(mold_component)]] | Broader than the simpler “section shaping the inside” entry in Autodesk; preserve the source-specific scope. |
| ゲート | ゲート | Entry through which molten resin is injected into the cavity; forms differ by function. | [[Gate_(injection_molding)]] | The entry does not provide an exhaustive subtype taxonomy. |
| スプル | スプル | Tapered section from machine nozzle to runner; the term may also refer to the material solidified there. | [[Sprue_(injection_molding)]] | Source records a passage sense and a solidified-material sense; do not add the latter as an alias. |
| ランナ | ランナ | Segment of the molten-resin path between sprue and gate; the word may also refer to solidified material in the segment. | [[Runner_(injection_molding)]] | Keep passage and leftover-material senses distinct in future usage records. |
| コールドスラグ; コールドスラグウエル | コールドスラグ | Cold/high-viscosity leading material; the well is the space at the sprue end or runner end that collects it. | [[Cold_Slug_Well]] | The index/term distinction between the material and the receiving volume is useful; only the latter is a mold structure. |
| ロケートリング | ロケートリング | A ring at the mold-to-machine interface, paired in the glossary context with locating/alignment terminology. | [[Locating_Ring]] | Compare its exact fit and scope with the JEED training usage; do not infer standard dimensions from the glossary. |
| アンダーカット | アンダーカット | Product portion that cannot be released without deformation or a special mold structure. | [[Undercut_(injection_molding)]] | A product-shape condition is described through its release consequence. |
| アンギュラーピン | アンギュラーピン | An inclined pin moves the slide core as the mold opens/closes to release an undercut. | [[Angled_Pin_(injection_molding)]]; [[Slide_Core_(injection_molding)]] | Confirm the English preferred term and keep the moving component distinct from the product undercut. |
