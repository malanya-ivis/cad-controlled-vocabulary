## Purpose and maintenance observations added 2026-10-06

The NLM preface explains that one subject list was chosen for book cataloging and periodical indexing to simplify the scheme for users and the work of maintaining it. It describes MeSH as a dynamic list with procedures for recommending and considering new headings, using terminology found in the literature. The current introduction says existing descriptors are checked before adding one, subject specialists maintain the vocabulary, and changes are released annually. This is a summary of the linked NLM pages; the detailed internal review workflow was not established.
