## Collected terminology

| Original label | Section / figure | Usage in context | Candidate concept | Open question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cavity | English entry “Cavity” | The region or space within the mold into which plastic is injected to form a plastic part. | [[Cavity_(injection_molding)]] | The entry is functional and injection-molding-specific; do not merge it with a generic CAD void or a mold component without more evidence. |
| Core | English entry “Core” | The section of the mold that shapes the inside of a molded part. | [[Core_(mold_component)]] | Confirm how this sense relates to a core side, core insert, and other mold-component uses. |
| Core pin | English and Japanese entries | A steel rod protruding into the cavity to create a through or blind hole in a part; the diameter can be changed by replacing the pin. | [[Core_Pin]] | A core pin is a mold component, not the resulting Hole. |
| Cooling channel / 冷却管 | English “Cooling channel”; Japanese “冷却管” | A passageway in the mold that circulates water or another cooling medium; channels are typically connected into serial or parallel circuits. | [[Cooling_Channel]] | The same geometry may look like a Hole; function requires mold-design context. |
| Gate / ゲート | English “Gate”; Japanese “ゲート” | A flow passage from the runner to the cavity; the glossary notes that it is generally small and solidifies first. | [[Gate_(injection_molding)]] | The term has a specific injection-molding flow-system meaning here. |
| Runner and Runner system / ランナーおよびランナー システム | English “Runner and Runner system”; Japanese “ランナーおよびランナー システム” | Channels feed polymer from the sprue to gates at the cavities; the system includes runners, sprue, and gates. | [[Runner_(injection_molding)]]; [[Runner_System_(injection_molding)]] | Keep the system grouping distinct from an individual flow passage. |
| Sprue / スプルー | English “Sprue”; Japanese “スプルー” in related entries | The main feed channel from the molding-machine nozzle to the runner system or directly to the part. | [[Sprue_(injection_molding)]] | Compare the passage sense with any source-specific solidified-material use; do not merge the residue with the passage. |
