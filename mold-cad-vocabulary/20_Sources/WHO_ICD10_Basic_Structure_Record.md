## Selected structural observations

Section 2.4 describes ICD as a practical classification with hierarchical subdivisions and multiple axes. Its structure balances different purposes and organizing principles. The WHO overview identifies ICD-10 as a prior release and states that ICD-11 came into effect on 2022-01-01; ICD-10 is used here because it was named as a structure example, not as the current WHO classification.

## Use in this project

Compare classification levels and boundary notes as an organizational example. Do not transfer disease categories, coding purposes, or a medical classification's axes to mold-CAD geometry.

## Purpose and ICD-10 revision example added 2026-10-06

WHO Volume 2 section 2.1 states that ICD supports systematic recording, analysis, interpretation, and comparison of mortality and morbidity data across places and time. Section 6.9 describes ICD-10 preparation: alternative structures were explored by collaborating centers, ICD-9 was evaluated, and consultation with countries and organizations contributed to the lengthy revision process. This describes the ICD-10 historical revision case, not a universal workflow for vocabularies.
