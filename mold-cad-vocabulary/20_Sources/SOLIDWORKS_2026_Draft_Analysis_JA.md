## Relevant wording and project use

The Japanese help explains that Draft Analysis checks molded-part faces against a selected opening/pull direction and a reference draft angle. Both the direction and angle must be supplied for the analysis. This supports recording the reference direction alongside an angle and face reference; it does not establish a universal sign convention or a project-wide geometry test.

A face that merely looks slanted in an STL is therefore not enough to label a verified Draft Angle unless the relevant pull/reference direction and angle convention are known. Record the observed slant as geometry and leave the mold-draft interpretation unconfirmed when those references are missing.

## Limits

This is a vendor analysis description, not a neutral standard definition. It does not establish terminology for all mold types, the project's preferred Japanese label, or an undercut conclusion for an arbitrary part.
