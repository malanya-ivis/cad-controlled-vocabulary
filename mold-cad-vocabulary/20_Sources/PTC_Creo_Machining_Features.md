## Collected terminology

| Original label | Section / figure | Usage in context | Candidate concept | Open question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Machining feature | Opening paragraph | A manufacturing feature describes material to remove from stock to achieve reference-model geometry; a closed removal volume is treated as a separate feature. | Operation term; not a new concept note | Keep this context separate from a general CAD structural feature. |
| Pocket | Listed feature types | A closed machining volume with a hard floor and hard walls; the floor may contain protruding bosses (islands). | [[Pocket]] | Does the project's CAD vocabulary need this machining-specific sense, a modeling-feature sense, or both? |
| Through Pocket | Listed feature types | A pocket-like closed machining volume with a soft floor and hard walls. | Candidate attribute combination; not a new note | Do not treat “Through” as a separate feature without a broader definition. |
| Slot | Listed feature types | An elongated machining volume with a hard floor, hard walls, and full radii on two opposite ends. | [[Slot]] | This is a machining definition and may be narrower than a general structural use. |
| Through Slot | Listed feature types | An elongated machining volume with a soft floor; the wall conditions differ from Slot. | Candidate attribute combination; not a new note | Confirm whether the CAD sample uses this term as a structure or an operation. |
| Floor / Hard Wall / Soft Wall | Opening paragraph and feature list | Boundary terminology describing the bottom and side surfaces of the material-removal volume and whether they belong to the reference model or stock. | Candidate terms; no concept notes yet | Record as operation vocabulary unless another source makes them independent CAD concepts. |
| Hole Group / Entry Hole | Listed feature types | A hole pattern to be drilled, or a predrilled hole used for tool entry into a closed feature. | Operation terms; not a new concept note | A machining entry hole must not be merged with the general Hole concept. |
| Top Chamfer / Top Round | Listed feature types | Material removal along chamfered or rounded edge chains; the resulting surfaces become the feature floor. | [[Chamfer]] / [[Fillet]] as distinguished source uses | These labels support a boundary note but do not define the general edge-treatment concepts. |
