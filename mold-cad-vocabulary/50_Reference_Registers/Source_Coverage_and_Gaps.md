## Main evidence gaps

1. [[Cross_Mold_Domain_Transfer]] now compares selected injection, casting and press-source terms. A cross-domain set of actual mold CAD samples is still missing; source comparison does not remove that gap.
2. [[Shape_Comparison_Examples]] now also includes a supplied Rib/Boss pair and [[CAD_Fillet_Boss_Name_Check]]. Closed/open void examples exist as explicitly synthetic teaching models. Drawing-term alignment, a verified enclosed void in a supplied single part and representative industrial mold parts remain gaps. [[Targeted_CAD_Gap_Examples]] records the screen of all 93 models and three unconfirmed nested-bound candidates.
3. ISO 25964 and JIS Z 8114 are represented by catalog/abstract reading only; term-level definitions require authorized clause-level access.
4. JDMIA member search remains inaccessible without member credentials; no attempt was made to bypass it.
5. Only four MISUMI ejection figures (#141 Figures 2–3; #142 Figures 2–3) have been visually inspected and summarized; other source figures remain uninspected. No figures or PDFs were copied into `80_Assets/`.
6. Japanese equivalents outside the same-version Inventor glossary remain source labels or candidates, not project aliases.
7. The original studies contain four simple and four complex detailed examples. [[Supplemental_Feature_Records.json]] adds three supplied-model records, so eleven supplied models have detailed draft records; two generated teaching models are separate. All 93 complex models have previews and shell screening, but only four have detailed semantic annotations. Mold-design roles, physical units, design intent and native CAD history remain unconfirmed.
8. [[Claim_Evidence_Matrix]] records the source limits of all fifteen core concepts. General Boss and Internal Void scope is still insufficiently supported. [[RAG_Search_Verification_Cases]] provides nineteen record-filter examples, not CAD recognition, a database implementation or RAG evaluation.
9. [[CAD_Geometry_Representation_and_Evidence]] proposes separating vocabulary concepts, observed geometry, and design specifications. STEP/B-rep and tolerance annotations have not yet been tested on a native CAD example; the current detailed geometry cases remain STL-based drafts.
