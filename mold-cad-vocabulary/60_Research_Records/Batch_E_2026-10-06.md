## Findings and boundaries

- Inventor calls a perpendicular rib a ウェブ in this help page; the source does not establish that リブ and ウェブ are project-wide synonyms.
- The Rib command can add Boss geometry at sketch points along the rib profile and lets the user set a diameter, offset, and draft angle. This documents one software-specific relationship, not a universal Boss subtype or attachment rule.
- The thickness, direction, depth, and taper controls are command parameters. Keep them separate from measured properties recorded on a part occurrence.
