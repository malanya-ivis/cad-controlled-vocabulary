## Findings and boundaries

- Draft angle needs a recorded pull/reference direction. Creo and Onshape describe CAD commands and their parameters; they do not establish one universal sign convention.
- Onshape Draft Analysis reports undercut regions relative to a selected mold-split direction. Retain this as direction-sensitive evidence, not a complete cross-vendor criterion.
- Creo describes a profile rib as a thin fin/web protrusion and says it is usually intended to strengthen parts or prevent bending. Record purpose on an individual feature only when that part's documentation or analysis supports it.
- Creo Shell can create a closed hollow result or an opened result and permits selected local thickness values. This clarifies the distinction among the operation, the resulting internal void, and wall-thickness values.
- No new concept nodes were added; existing definitions remain drafts.

## Evidence limits

Only the stated help-page text was read. CAD command terminology is usage evidence, not a software-independent definition or proof that an instance has a particular purpose. No source images or full help text were copied.
