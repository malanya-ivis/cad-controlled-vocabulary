## Working conclusions

1. A shared label may refer to a region, component, system, or solidified process residue. The concept boundary must say which reading is intended.
2. A connected route or function (cooling, melt delivery, release) cannot be inferred from shape alone.
3. Do not use an index, catalog abstract, or a single software glossary to promote a draft to `checked`.
4. Japanese labels remain in source notes. This comparison does not authorize new YAML aliases.

## Open decisions

- This comparison records injection-molding source usage only; add evidence from other mold types before generalizing its boundaries.
- Decide whether separate notes are needed for female cavity components, core inserts, solidified runners, cooling circuits, and mold-release mechanisms.
- Check the relevant authorized standards or teacher-provided documents if a standards-based definition is required.
