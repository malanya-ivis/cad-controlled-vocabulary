# JST Science and Technology Thesaurus

**Study status:** Supplementary comparison; not identified as a core reference in the supervisor clarification.

## Purpose and scope

JST describes its Science and Technology Thesaurus as a systematic dictionary of specialist terms across science and technology, used as the backbone for literature indexing and search. A larger dictionary adds synonyms, spelling variants, and links to broader or closely related thesaurus terms. The reported 2017 revision is especially useful for understanding term replacement and legacy-index maintenance in a Japanese technical context.

## Application to mold CAD

- What to adopt and why: record source usage before choosing a preferred name; preserve replaced or unconfirmed expressions as candidate labels or questions; when a concept changes, record the reason and update links deliberately rather than silently deleting the old wording.
- What not to adopt directly and why: do not use frequency counts alone to decide whether a CAD term is structurally valid, and do not assume that a search thesaurus's legacy remapping solves geometric or functional definition conflicts.
- Limits of this investigation: one Japanese article about the 2017 revision was read; the current JST browser, full large-dictionary schema, and later revision procedures were not investigated.
