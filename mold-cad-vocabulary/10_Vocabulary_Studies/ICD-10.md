# ICD-10

**Study status:** Structure example named by the supervisor; a limited WHO Volume 2 section was reviewed.

## Purpose and scope

ICD-10 is a WHO classification of diseases and related health conditions. Its structure is intended for classification and reporting, not geometric description. The WHO Volume 2 section reviewed describes hierarchical subdivision and practical classification choices.
