# ANSI/NISO Z39.19: Monolingual Controlled Vocabularies

**Study status:** Selected sections read; supervisor-identified method reference; selected sections read, not a complete study.

## Purpose and scope

ANSI/NISO Z39.19 gives guidance for constructing, formatting, managing, and maintaining monolingual controlled vocabularies. The investigation focused on selected sections about warrant, term selection, compound terms, relationships, and maintenance. It is a methods reference; it does not supply injection-molding definitions.

## Application to mold CAD

- Adopt source warrants as a way to describe why a candidate term is present: use in an authoritative standard or manual, actual user or industry usage, and project need should be recorded separately.
- Distinguish a homograph qualifier (for example, `Core (mold component)`) from a scope note that says what the term means in this vault.
- Treat compound-term decisions as project policy. Do not split every phrase or treat every multiword name as a single irreducible concept.
- Record revision reasons and dates in the vault's research log rather than implementing a standards-compliant thesaurus platform.
- Do not use frequency or any single warrant as proof that a CAD or mold term has one universal meaning.
