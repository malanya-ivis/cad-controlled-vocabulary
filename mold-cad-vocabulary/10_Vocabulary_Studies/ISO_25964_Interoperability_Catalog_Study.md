# ISO 25964: Thesauri and Interoperability with Other Vocabularies

**Study status:** Supervisor-identified method reference. Official catalog records for Parts 1 and 2 reviewed; neither full standard was read.

## Purpose and scope

- **Part 1** concerns thesauri for information retrieval and recommendations for their development and maintenance. The published 2011 edition is listed as current in its 2022 review metadata; a second edition was in the approval/publication process when checked 2026-10-06.
- **Part 2** concerns interoperability with other vocabularies and mapping maintenance. The published 2013 edition was confirmed in 2023; an updated second edition is under development when checked 2026-10-06.

Catalog status can change. Recheck the official records before citing an edition as current. These notes report catalog metadata and abstracts only, not detailed requirements.
