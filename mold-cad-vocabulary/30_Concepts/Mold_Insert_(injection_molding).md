# Mold Insert (injection molding)

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Project label candidate / source-supported draft] A separately made mold portion fitted into the Mold Base; JEED describes the `入れ子` sense as a divided cavity-forming portion placed into the base. MISUMI's cavity/core articles also discuss integral versus split construction. MISUMI separately uses `removable insert` for an undercut-forming insert that leaves the mold with the part and is manually removed/reinstalled; keep that semi-automatic arrangement distinct unless a source establishes it as a subtype of this base-fitted concept. The English label is a project candidate; no Japanese alias is asserted.

## Broader concept

Not established. The sources do not define a complete insert taxonomy.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Cavity_(injection_molding)|Cavity]] and [[Core_(mold_component)|Core]]: forming regions/components that may be constructed integrally or in divided pieces.
- [[Mold_Base]]: receives/fits the insert in the JEED glossary usage.
- [[Core_Pin]]: a specific pin-shaped forming component; this note describes a broader divided mold portion.
- `Removable insert`: a source-described undercut-release arrangement, not assumed synonymous with a base-fitted insert.
