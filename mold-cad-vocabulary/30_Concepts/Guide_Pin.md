# Guide Pin

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A pin component that aligns the fixed and moving mold sides as the mold opens and closes. This note describes the alignment component, not a locating ring at the machine interface or a slide-driving angled pin.

## Broader concept

Not established. A broader mold-guidance assembly has not been defined.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Guide_Bush]]: guides the sliding movement of the Guide Pin.
- [[Locating_Ring]]: locates the mold relative to the molding machine; the Guide Pin aligns mold halves.
- [[Angled_Pin_(injection_molding)|Angled Pin]]: drives a Slide Core rather than aligning the fixed/moving mold sides.
