# Mold (injection molding)

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A metal tool used to produce repeated parts of the same shape; the JEED glossary describes the product as being formed in the mold's Cavity portion. This note uses Mold for the whole forming tool/assembly and keeps it distinct from the Mold Base, which some sources use for a supporting plate set and others describe more broadly as the mold body.

## Broader concept

Not established. The mold's complete component and subsystem hierarchy has not been established from the selected sources.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Mold_Base]]: a supporting assembly or plate set within the whole mold in MISUMI's scoped use; JEED also records a broader Mold Base sense that approaches the whole mold.
- [[Cavity_(injection_molding)|Cavity]]: the source-selected forming-space sense is where the molded product takes shape; JEED uses `キャビティ部` differently in separate entries, as recorded in that note.
- [[Ejector_Plate]] and [[Ejector_Pin]]: mold-side ejection components in the selected JEED training source.
- [[Sprue_(injection_molding)|Sprue]], [[Runner_(injection_molding)|Runner]], and [[Gate_(injection_molding)|Gate]]: feed-path concepts associated with the mold; they are not the whole Mold.
