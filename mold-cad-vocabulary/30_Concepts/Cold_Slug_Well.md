# Cold Slug Well

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A receiving space at a sprue or runner end that collects the colder, higher-viscosity leading material so it does not enter the cavity with the rest of the melt. This note names the receiving volume, not the cold material itself.

## Broader concept

Not established.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Sprue_(injection_molding)|Sprue]] and [[Runner_(injection_molding)|Runner]]: sources place the well at a sprue or runner end.
- [[Cavity_(injection_molding)|Cavity]]: the cold leading material is held away from the cavity.
- [[Runner_System_(injection_molding)|Runner system]]: the well is associated with the feed system but is not one of the three elements Autodesk lists in its system entry.
