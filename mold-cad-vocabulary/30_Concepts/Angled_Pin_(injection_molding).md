# Angled Pin (injection molding)

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Project label candidate / source-supported draft] An inclined mold pin that drives a Slide Core to release an undercut. JEED's glossary and Shibaura's glossary support this functional use; the English preferred name remains provisional and is not added as a synonym for an unverified Japanese translation.

## Broader concept

Not established. A broader moving-core drive mechanism has not been defined.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Slide_Core_(injection_molding)|Slide Core]]: component driven by the angled pin.
- [[Undercut_(injection_molding)|Undercut]]: product-release condition addressed by the moving core; not a component.
- [[Guide_Pin]]: aligns mold sides and has a different role.
