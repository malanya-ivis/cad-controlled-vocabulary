# Sprue (injection molding)

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] The tapered feed passage that carries melt from the molding-machine nozzle toward the runner system or, in some layouts, directly toward the cavity. This note uses the passage sense. Shibaura also uses スプル for the solidified material in the passage; that material sense is recorded as a source ambiguity, not as an alias or part of this concept definition.

## Broader concept

Not established. The sources support a feed-system relationship but do not provide a project-approved hierarchy.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Runner_(injection_molding)|Runner]]: connected downstream feed path; Shibaura defines Runner as the path section between Sprue and Gate.
- [[Runner_System_(injection_molding)|Runner system]]: a source-defined system that may include Sprue, runners, and gates.
- [[Gate_(injection_molding)|Gate]]: downstream entrance from runner to cavity.
- [[Mold_Base]]: a sprue may be formed in or supported by a component assembly, but the passage and assembly are distinct.
