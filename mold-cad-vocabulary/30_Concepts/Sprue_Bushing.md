# Sprue Bushing

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A replaceable mold component used to form or provide the sprue passage at the interface with the molding-machine nozzle. JEED explains that the nozzle contact can cause wear and describes using a separately made, replaceable bushing rather than carving the sprue directly into the mold.

## Broader concept

Not established. A broader mold-feed component class has not been selected.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Sprue_(injection_molding)|Sprue]]: the flow passage associated with the bushing; the bushing is the component, the sprue is the passage.
- [[Runner_(injection_molding)|Runner]]: downstream feed path connected to the sprue.
- [[Mold_Base]]: the bushing may be mounted within the mold-base assembly.
