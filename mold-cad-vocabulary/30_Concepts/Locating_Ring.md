# Locating Ring

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A mold component at the machine interface that positions the mold in relation to the injection-molding-machine platen/nozzle. JEED describes fitting the locating ring to the platen hole; Shibaura includes a locating-ring entry in its injection-molding glossary. This note records the alignment component, not the locating hole, its dimensions, or all machine-mounting hardware.

## Broader concept

Not established. The project has not defined a general mold-machine interface assembly.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Mold_Base]]: the ring is installed at the mold/base interface; it is a component, not the entire supporting assembly.
- [[Sprue_Bushing]]: both are near the machine-side feed interface, but one locates the mold and the other forms/provides the sprue passage.
- [[Hole]]: a locating/platen hole is a receiving feature; the ring is a separate component.
