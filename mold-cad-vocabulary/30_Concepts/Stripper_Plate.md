# Stripper Plate

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A plate that strips a molded article from the main Core by advancing around its side surface. In the MISUMI arrangement, a connecting rod links the Stripper Plate to the Ejector Plate, whose motion advances and returns it. This is the product-contacting stripping member, distinct from the plate that drives it.

## Broader concept

Not established. A broader ejection-assembly hierarchy has not been defined.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Ejector_Plate]]: supplies motion through a connecting rod in the cited stripper-plate arrangement.
- [[Core_(mold_component)|Core]]: the main core remains fixed while the Stripper Plate moves around its side surface in the cited arrangement.
- [[Ejector_Pin]]: pin ejection can leave marks; the source presents stripper-plate ejection as an option when such marks should be avoided.
