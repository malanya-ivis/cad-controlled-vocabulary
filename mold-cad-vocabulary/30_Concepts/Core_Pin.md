# Core Pin

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A pin-shaped steel mold component that projects into the cavity to form a through-hole or blind hole in the molded part. The pin is a mold component; the hole is the resulting product structure. MISUMI also documents a specialized gas-venting use in which a core pin's clearance and side-surface vent/groove provide an escape path for cavity gas; this is a secondary design role, not the general definition of every Core Pin.

## Broader concept

Not established. The sources do not define a complete hierarchy of core parts and inserts.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Core_(mold_component)|Core]]: related mold-forming component, but a Core Pin is a narrower pin-shaped component in the cited glossary.
- [[Hole]]: the molded feature created by the pin; the resulting Hole is not the pin itself.
- [[Cavity_(injection_molding)|Cavity]]: the pin protrudes into the forming region in the Autodesk glossary's description.
