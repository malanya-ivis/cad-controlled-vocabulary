# Ejector Pin

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A pin component in an injection mold used to push a molded product out during ejection. JEED distinguishes pin forms and notes that clearance around ejector pins may also let mold gases escape. That secondary venting role does not make the pin itself a Gas Vent, and this note keeps the general component separate from possible pin subtypes.

## Broader concept

Not established. A broader ejector-system concept has not been selected.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Mold_Base]]: ejector plates and pins are part of the described supporting/ejection assembly.
- [[Ejector_Plate]]: plate in the ejection assembly used together with pins; the pin is the product-contacting component in this draft.
- [[Stripper_Plate]]: an alternative plate-based ejection member is described for designs where ejector-pin marks should be avoided; retain the component distinction.
- [[Core_Pin]]: distinguished by role in the cited sources; a core pin shapes a hole, while an ejector pin pushes the product out.
- [[Cold_Slug_Well]]: not an ejection component; it retains cold leading melt in the feed system.
- [[Gas_Vent_(injection_mold)|Gas Vent]]: gas may escape through ejector-pin clearance in the JEED glossary; the pin is not equivalent to the vent groove/function.
