# Runner System (injection molding)

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A mold feed-system grouping that distributes melt toward one or more Cavity regions. Autodesk explicitly lists runners, Sprue, and Gate(s) in its `Runner and Runner system` entry; JEED's glossary likewise groups スプルー, ランナー, and ゲート under `ランナーシステム部`. This note records the source-supported group, not a universal design boundary or an individual Runner, Sprue, or Gate.

## Broader concept

Not established. The parts-to-whole relation is recorded below rather than as a kind-of hierarchy.
