# Guide Bush

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A bushing component that guides a moving Guide Pin during mold opening and closing. The source describes it by its relation to the pin; this note does not import material or hardness recommendations as part of the concept definition.

## Broader concept

Not established.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Guide_Pin]]: the sliding pin guided by this bushing.
- [[Locating_Ring]]: a different mold-to-machine positioning component.
