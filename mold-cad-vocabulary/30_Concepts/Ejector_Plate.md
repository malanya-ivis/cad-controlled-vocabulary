# Ejector Plate

> Context only: retained from the earlier mold-component/process study; this note is outside the active single-part shape-feature set.


## Definition and scope

[Source-supported draft / injection-molding scope] A plate in the mold ejection assembly that moves to transmit ejection motion. JEED describes plate-and-pin ejection; MISUMI #141 shows the Ejector Plate driving a separate Stripper Plate through a connecting rod, and #142 describes a configuration with a Runner Ejecting Plate nested within the Ejector Plate. MISUMI lists ejector plates among mold-base components and separately describes an ejector guide system for smooth plate travel. These are source-specific configurations, not an exhaustive assembly definition; the machine-side ejector rod remains outside this note.

## Broader concept

Not established. The relation between ejector plate, retainer plate, and the full ejector assembly needs task-specific granularity.

## Related or distinguished concepts

- [[Ejector_Pin]]: pin components act on the product; the plate belongs to the supporting/ejection assembly.
- [[Stripper_Plate]]: a separate product-stripping member can be driven by the Ejector Plate through a connecting rod.
- [[Mold_Base]]: MISUMI lists the ejector plate as a mold-base component.
- [[JEED_Plastic_Mold_Glossary_Index]] records an Ejector Rod as injection-machine hardware that pushes the mold's ejector plate; no machine-side concept note is created in this mold-component batch.
